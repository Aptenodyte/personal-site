Honestly, I have a few reasons for wanting to start a blog. Firstly, I would like to prevent my already questionable writing skills from decaying even further; if I can write about tech stuff I'm interested in in the process, all the better. Secondly, I occasionally work on (at least what I think are) cool side projects and CTFs, and I'd like a place to show off some of my work. With any luck, this might also motivate me to actually finish my projects instead of getting bored of them halfway through... :P

Beyond that though, I hope at least some people will be able to benefit from what I write. We live in a time where our privacy and fourth amendment rights are constantly being threatened in ways that most people aren't aware of, and I believe the situation is only going to worsen (at least for a time) with the proliferation of mass surveillance which is happening right in front of our faces. I hope that some of my posts can maybe draw a bit of attention to that. Knowledge is power, and people more informed of how their privacy is being infringed upon will definitely be better equipped to defend their rights.

Oh yeah, I also really like thinking out loud, especially when I'm learning new things. Writing out my thoughts will force me to actually comprehensively understand what I'm learning, and will probably help me fill in my own mental gaps decent bit. With any luck, they might help out someone else trying to learn the same thing too.

Anyways, enough yapping, and I hope y'all enjoy my writing :)
